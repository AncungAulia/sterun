Race day: QR pass and scanner

STE-18. Screens for STE-21 (runner pass) and STE-22 (scanner PWA). Every value comes from the design tokens; the QR codes are real payloads from docs/specs/HASH_AND_TOTP.md §5. The written spec is docs/design/race-day/README.md.

Runner: the pass (STE-21, /pass/[tokenId])

One screen, four states. The bib is the largest thing on it, because at a pickup desk the volunteer reads the bib before anything else; the QR and the six digits are two routes to the same check.

Volunteer: the scanner (STE-22, /scan)

A verdict has to be readable at arm's length, in sunlight, in under two seconds. Green and red are 1.26:1 apart in luminance, which is nothing, so the icon shape, the word and the striped ground on a refusal carry the meaning, and the colour only confirms it.
