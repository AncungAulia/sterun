Race day: motion

Seven studies for STE-18, at the durations and curves in the motion tokens. The rule they follow: movement may make a change legible, never delay it. A verdict is readable before its own animation has finished, and the QR itself never animates, because a code that is mid-fade is a code a camera can misread. Flip reduced motion to check that every screen still says the same thing with nothing moving.

Each frame also has its own Replay.
