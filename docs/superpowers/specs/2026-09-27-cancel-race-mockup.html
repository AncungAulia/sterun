Cancelling a race

The console can open entries, close them and reopen them. It has never been able to cancel, and that was deliberate in STE-17. The gap shows the moment somebody publishes a race by mistake: today there is no way to withdraw it, and a name on chain cannot be edited.

No contract work. `set_event_status` has accepted `Cancelled` since v2, from Draft, Open or Closed. What is missing is the screen, and the care around it.

This is the only action in the console that cannot be undone. Cancelled is terminal on chain: the race cannot reopen, cannot be corrected, and cannot be deleted. Everything below follows from that one sentence.

SCREEN 1

Where it lives

At the bottom of the menu, under a rule, in the danger colour. Never the button that leads: nobody opens a race page in order to cancel it.

One line per item, with its own mark (Ancung, 2026-09-27). The explanatory second lines are gone: a menu is a list of things to do, and a sentence under each one turns choosing into reading. Lucide, matching what each does: CalendarClock for the date, Lock for closing and LockOpen for reopening, UserPlus for adding entries, CircleX for cancelling.

Why not a button

The header's one button is whatever a race needs next, and no race ever needs cancelling next. Putting it there would also place the one irreversible action where the pointer already is.

It is offered on Draft, Open and Closed, the three the contract accepts. A race already Completed or Cancelled has no menu at all.

The marks are wayfinding rather than decoration: in a list where two items both stop entries, the shape tells them apart before the words are read. The cancel one is the only red thing in the console.

SCREEN 2

The confirmation, when people have paid

The numbers come from the chain, not from the index, and they are the whole point of this screen: an organiser about to cancel should read what it costs other people before they read the button.

Cancel Merdeka Run 2026?

412 runners have entered, and they have paid 4,120 sUSD.

Sterun never held that money: it went straight to your wallet as each runner entered. Cancelling does not send any of it back, and no refund can be made from here.

  • Entries stop at once and cannot reopen
  • Every entry stays on each runner's record, marked "Race cancelled"
  • The race page stays online, saying the race was cancelled
  • Nobody, including us, can undo this

Screen 2. The confirm button stays dead until the name matches. The way out is "Keep the race", not "Cancel", because two buttons both meaning cancel is how people press the wrong one.

Why typing the name

Every other dialog in this console guards a signature that can be undone by another signature. This one cannot be undone by anything. Typing the name is the only guard that scales with the cost, and it is the pattern people already know from deleting a repository.

Why say the money out loud

Because there is no escrow. The fee moved from runner to organiser at the moment of entry, so a cancelled race leaves the organiser holding money for a race that will not happen. That is a promise they now owe off chain, and a screen that stays quiet about it is helping somebody forget.

There is no delete, and the screen says so

Cancelling hides a race from the directory, which is what "make it go away" means in practice. What it does not do is remove anything: the page keeps its URL for ever, a search still reaches it, and the name stays on chain.

Saying that here is the difference between an organiser who is surprised later and one who is not.

SCREEN 4

Afterwards, on both sides

The console keeps the race, because it still has entries, results and a history worth reading. The directory drops it. The runner's record keeps the entry and says what happened to it.
