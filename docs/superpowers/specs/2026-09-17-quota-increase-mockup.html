Adding places to a distance

STE-57. An organiser raises a distance's places from the race console, and runners see when and why. Raising places is paired with a signed announcement: runners paid against the published number, so a change to it is said in public.

1. Where the button lives

In the race header, top right, as a menu of distances

The header's action changes with the race's status. Close entries has to stay: until entries close on their own at the registration end date (STE-46, not built yet), it is the only thing that stops registration. So an open race shows two buttons, and adding places is the main one.

2. The dialog

One form: the new number and the announcement together

The first line of the announcement is written by the page from the numbers, so it always matches what changes. The organiser can add a note under it. Nothing is sent until "Add places and announce".

3. After pressing

Two approvals in the wallet, then the announcement goes up

The announcement is approved first, before the places change, so the raise and its announcement never drift apart. The dialog cannot be closed while it runs.

5. What runners see on /events/[id]

The raise on the distance, and an Updates list

The distance card says when places were added and from what. The Details tab gets an Updates section with every announcement, newest first. A tick says the organiser really signed it; if it cannot be confirmed, it says so rather than hiding the announcement.
